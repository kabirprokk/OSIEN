SELF-SERVE

Before you write in

My email link expired — what now?
Links last 24h (reset: 1h). In the app go to Sign in → resend. The new email replaces the old link.
Crew isn't responding to my booking?
Paid requests go to nearby available crew, and the studio sees bookings that get stuck. If yours ever does, email us the booking code and we'll step in.
How do refunds arrive?
To the original payment method. >48h: full · 24–48h: 50% · <24h: no refund. Studio-cancelled: always full.
How do I delete my data?
Profile → Delete Account in-app, or follow the deletion page. Done within 30 days.
