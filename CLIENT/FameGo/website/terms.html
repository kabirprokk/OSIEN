01What FameGo is

FameGo is a marketplace that connects clients who need shoot crew with crew (cinematographers, photographers, videographers, drone operators, editors, assistants), overseen by studio admins. FameBros Studio operates the platform, verifies crew, and monitors bookings — it does not itself operate cameras unless explicitly assigned.

02Accounts & roles

  • You must be 18+ (or supervised by a guardian) and provide accurate name, email and phone.
  • One account per person. CLIENT, CREW and ADMIN roles are assigned by the system; misrepresenting your role leads to suspension.
  • Crew profiles require city, primary role, experience, gear and portfolio. Status is VERIFIED, PENDING_VERIFICATION or SUSPENDED — only VERIFIED crew receive paid requests.
  • You are responsible for activity under your account. Report unauthorised use immediately.

03Bookings

  • A booking is created when a client completes the 6-step flow (what / when / where / who / details / confirm) and pays the plan price.
  • Statuses follow: Draft → Finding Crew → Confirmed → Upcoming → In Progress → Completed, or Cancelled.
  • Clients must give accurate venue, time and access instructions. Wrong addresses or denied entry count as client no-show.
  • Crew must arrive ready (charged gear, agreed kit) at least 15 minutes early and follow the shoot brief and venue rules.

04Plans & payments

  • Plans: Bronze 90-min ₹1,999 · Silver 3-hr ₹2,999 · Gold 6-hr ₹8,999 (inclusive of buffer). Prices include GST where applicable and are shown before you pay.
  • Current builds use demo payments; when live UPI/cards launch, the same sections apply and receipts are issued per booking.
  • Crew payouts are settled by the studio per assignment; crew agree not to solicit off-platform payment for FameGo bookings.

05Cancellations & refunds

  • Client cancels >48 hrs before arrival: full refund to source.
  • 24–48 hrs before: 50% refund; crew keep a mobilisation fee.
  • <24 hrs or no-show: no refund (crew were reserved and turned down other work).
  • If FameGo cannot find crew or cancels from our side: full refund, no questions.
  • Reschedules requested >24 hrs ahead are free once, subject to crew availability.
