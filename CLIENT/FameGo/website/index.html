10 / FINE PRINT, PLAIN WORDS

Asked often.

Is the app free to download?
Yes. You pay only when you confirm a shoot plan. Browsing, support and chat on an active booking are free.
How fast will crew respond?
Paid requests go to nearby available crew instantly, and the studio monitors bookings that get stuck. If yours ever does, email us the booking code and we'll step in.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes, from Booking Details. Over 48 hours ahead: full refund. 24–48 hours: half. Under 24 hours: no refund, because the crew turned down other work. Full policy in Terms §5.
How does crew verification work?
ID, portfolio, experience, gear and location — five checks. Only verified profiles receive paid requests, and status is always visible.
Which phones are supported?
Android 7.0 and above. Push notifications need Google Play services.
How do I delete my account?
In-app: Profile → Delete Account. Or email famebros.studio@gmail.com with “Delete my FameGo account”. Details →
