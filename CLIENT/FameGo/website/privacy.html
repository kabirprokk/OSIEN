Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026 · Controller: FameBros Studio · famebros.studio@gmail.com · Backend: Supabase (Postgres + Auth)

01What we collect

  • Account: name, email, phone, company, role, avatar initials.
  • Crew profiles: city, roles, experience, bio, gear, portfolio/Instagram links, availability, ratings.
  • Bookings: title, category, date/time, venue + address, crew requirements, plan, payment reference, chat messages, notifications.
  • Device: push token (FCM), crash reports, approximate location only during an active shoot when you allow it.

02Why we use it

To create accounts, match and notify crew, run booking chat and tracking, process payments and refunds, verify crew, prevent fraud, provide support, and improve reliability. We never sell personal data. Marketing emails are opt-in only.

03Sharing

  • Clients see assigned crew name, phone, gear and rating — and vice versa — only after confirmation.
  • Processors: Supabase (hosting/auth/db), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push), MapTiler (geocoding). Each sees only what it needs.
  • We disclose data when required by law or to protect safety and prevent fraud.

04Retention & deletion

Active data is kept while your account exists. Delete anytime in-app (Profile → Delete Account) or via email with subject “Delete my FameGo account”. We erase profile, crew data, locations, tokens and chats within 30 days, except payment/booking records the law requires us to keep (anonymised where possible). Backups age out within 90 days.

05Your rights & contact

Access, correct, export or restrict your data anytime by emailing famebros.studio@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. Location and notification permissions can be revoked in Android settings — the app keeps working, minus live tracking and push.
